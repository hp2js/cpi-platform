## Scoring baseline

The demo profile is **Hackathon Mock v1**: procedures 10, risk assessment 15, mitigation plan 15, implementation 60. The PRD records a conflict with the supplied published 23rd Cycle structure: assessment 10, plan 10, implementation 80, with procedures as a prerequisite (S02 versus S03). The demo profile must remain labelled as a simulation, not official EACC scoring. Matching the published weights alone would not validate an evaluator rubric.

PRD §10 defines the four binary checks per foundation, milestone credit, locked denominators, exact arithmetic and display rounding. Annual scoring counts foundations once and averages all four implementation quarters equally. A quarter awaiting disposition is pending; an explicitly finalized nonresponse may contribute zero. Late reporting is shown separately with no invented numerical penalty. The worked expectations 88.75, 70.00 and 96.25 require independent reconciliation in [HP2-11](https://linear.app/hp2js/issue/HP2-11) and [HP2-36](https://linear.app/hp2js/issue/HP2-36); listing them here is not acceptance evidence.

## Recording an answer and applying a change

Maintain each answer once, here, under its O-ID. Issue descriptions should link to it instead of copying mutable policy. Preserve the previous default and its reason in the change record when superseded.

For each answer, record:

- O-ID and status: Team baseline, Event details unspecified, Specific organizer rule, or Superseded.
- Exact answer or faithful summary, named source/author, source link or repository artifact, and date received.
- Applicability: hackathon demonstration, published cycle evaluation, or later production operation.
- Conflict with an existing source/default, the resulting prototype decision, and any remaining uncertainty.
- Affected PRD requirements and Linear issues, plus verification required before adoption.

A meeting invitation is not an answer. Record oral or relayed guidance with its provenance, as above, and apply it within its stated scope; do not turn broad discretion into invented judging, legal or licensing terms. Do not attach private messages or personal data to public artifacts.

Changes to O01–O03 or O09 require independent expected-result updates in HP2-11 before regression/acceptance in HP2-36. Changes to O04/O10 require deadline-boundary and response-window checks; O05–O08/O11 require submission, evidence and revision checks; O06/O07/O15/O17 require scope, publication and export checks. Apply changed rules through versioned profiles/forms and explicit correction paths, not retroactive mutation of historical records.

O12–O14 constrain the final judging package in HP2-37. They can remain explicit unknowns in this register without pretending that the event-readiness task is complete. Organizer confirmation also does not establish production readiness or resolve implementation defects.
