# Walkthrough task cards

Facilitator: reveal only the prompt, not the completion evidence, while the participant works. Use the fixture gates in [the guide](README.md). These are tasks to run, not recorded results.

| ID  | Participant    | Participant-facing prompt                                                                                                                                                                                       | Completion evidence for observer                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Trace            |

Record the situation honestly and submit what is available.          | Draft survives validation; authority confirmed; missing approval distinguished from missing evidence; deficiency stated without fabricated attachment; participant understands receipt does not mean accepted achievement.                                                     | AT26; FR05–07    |

What would need to happen to correct a published decision?                                                                        | Traces foundations and four quarterly dispositions; distinguishes pending from zero; explains authorized correction and retained prior release.                                                                                                                                | FR10, FR13       |

Ask every participant at debrief: “Does a receipt mean the institution has met the criteria?”, “What changes when a result is published?”, and “Can an uploaded document alone prove an activity happened?” Record their explanation faithfully rather than a prompted yes/no response.

For keyboard observation, offer R1/R2 or V1 using the participant's usual keyboard/assistive setup. Record the exact journey and devices tested. A partial keyboard task is not full accessibility conformance. Optional internal supervisor/admin rehearsal can inspect backlog, scope, publication and correction, alongside the reporting/reviewing tasks.
