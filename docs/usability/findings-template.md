## Severity and action

**Acceptance blocker:** wrong institution access, silent data loss, misunderstood deadline, or inability to explain an awarded point. Stop affected tasks; preserve evidence and retest after remediation.

**Major:** task cannot be completed unaided or requires a workaround likely to produce incorrect work. **Minor:** recoverable friction without incorrect outcome. A repeated confusion affecting two participants triggers design review regardless of initial severity. Record severity justification; do not turn a numerical label into the evidence.

Keep owner, delivery status and scheduling in Linear. Put the observation and retest evidence here. A feature request or domain disagreement is not automatically an implementation defect.
